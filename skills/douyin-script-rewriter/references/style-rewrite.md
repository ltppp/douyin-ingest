# Douyin口播原创改写规则

Use only after producing a usable `AI 校正版逐字稿`.

## Analyze the Source

Extract:

- Audience and immediate pain point.
- Opening hook and first-three-second promise.
- Main claim and supporting progression.
- Emotional baseline, escalation, and payoff.
- Sentence length, repetition, pauses, and speaking tempo.
- CTA type: reassurance, comment prompt, action instruction, blessing, follow, or reflection.
- Distinctive expressions that must not be copied.
- Unsupported guarantees or sensitive claims that require softening.

## Rewrite Method

1. State the reusable content mechanism in abstract terms.
2. Build a new opening that serves the same audience need without copying the source line.
3. Reorder or replace examples, transitions, and supporting details.
4. Preserve spoken momentum while changing the surface language and memorable phrasing.
5. End with a new payoff or CTA that matches the rewritten script.

## Originality Gate

Reject and rewrite the draft when any of these are true:

- It follows the source sentence by sentence.
- Most changes are synonyms or particle replacements.
- It retains a distinctive source sentence or metaphor.
- The opening and ending are materially the same as the source.
- It introduces facts, promises, or advice not supported by the source or user request.

## Speaking Quality Gate

The final script should:

- Sound natural when read aloud once.
- Use short, speakable sentences.
- Keep one main idea per sentence.
- Avoid written-language transitions and long explanatory paragraphs.
- Establish audience relevance immediately.
- End cleanly without an unnecessary summary of the tool or workflow.

## Sensitive Claims

Transform deterministic claims such as `一定复合`, `必然回来`, `保证发财`, or guaranteed health outcomes into emotional framing, reflection, or practical suggestions unless the user explicitly requests a clearly fictional style.
